    My schedule

    Photo or screenshot

    Choose a screenshot of your MyUCSC Class Schedule page (or a clear, straight-on photo of it), then read it. Check the classes it finds against your picture.

    This reads the text inside your browser, so the picture never leaves your computer and no account is needed. The first time, it downloads a text reader of about 10 MB.

    Or read it with Claude (needs an API key)

    Sends the picture to Anthropic's Claude API when you press the button; it is not stored. It is more forgiving of photos than the on-device reader.

    Calendar file (.ics)

    Import classes from a calendar you exported from Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Canvas or similar. Weekly classes, their end dates and skipped days are understood; anything it can't represent is listed rather than guessed. The file is read in your browser and only its text is sent to this app's own server.

    Paste from MyUCSC

    In MyUCSC open Enrollment, then Class Schedule. Select the whole page, copy, and paste it here. Instructor names are not kept.
