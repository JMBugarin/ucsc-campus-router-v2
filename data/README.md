# data/

- `building_sources.json` — hand-edited. One entry per building: `id`, display `name`, `osm_name` (exact OpenStreetMap name), and `facility_ids` (links the building to rooms in `classrooms.json`), and `aliases` (extra names; the official schedule names are added automatically). `osm_name: null` means no location is known yet.
- `classrooms.json` — generated by `data-pipeline/fetch_classrooms.py` from https://classrooms.ucsc.edu/ (each room's official schedule name, "AIS Display Name", and facility id) and the public classroom-points layer behind https://maps.ucsc.edu/ (each room's lat/lon and level).
- `buildings.json` — generated by `data-pipeline/build_buildings.py`. Adds `centroid` and `entrances` from OpenStreetMap. Do not hand-edit; change the sources file and regenerate.
- `rooms.json` — optional per-room overrides, keyed `"<building-id>:<ROOM>"`:

  ```json
  {"overrides": {"baskin-engineering:152": {"entrance": {"lat": 37.0, "lon": -122.06}, "floor": "1", "note": "use the north door"}}}
  ```
- `entry_point_sources.json` — hand-reviewed. The places a student can arrive at campus (campus bus stops and parking), each with an `id`, display `name`, `type` and the OpenStreetMap element (`node/…` or `way/…`) it comes from. OSM's gates and entrances are unnamed and were not included.
- `entry_points.json` — generated by `data-pipeline/build_entry_points.py` (standard library only). Adds each point's position and the walking-graph node it snaps to, and refuses a point outside the graph or more than 40 m from a path. Contains no timetable or route data. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
